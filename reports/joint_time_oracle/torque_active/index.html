Joint path-and-time optimization with active torque limits

Scope and qualification. Torque stress is synthetic uniform derating of asymmetric Panda actuator limits; it is not a physical payload experiment. The same 20 source tasks are tested in correlated moderate/severe profiles, not 40 independent tasks. Independent checks use aligned Pinocchio RNEA and analytic collision sphere primitives. Added samples surround observed extrema; no continuous-time collision or dynamic certificate is claimed. C results are feasible incumbents from a local optimizer and are not global optima.

Methods: A10/A40 native TimeOpt on the frozen cuRobo geometry and modified effort limits (10/40 iterations); B pointwise IPOPT timing on the original geometry; C SLSQP joint path/time with 14/22/34 controls and four starts per resolution; D the same IPOPT timing solver on C's exact quintic geometry. Primary geometry contrast: B versus D.

Solver diagnostics

C convergence was reported for 0 of 120 resolution attempts. Nonconverged but independently feasible candidates remain visible; independent feasibility does not establish local stationarity. B/D use the same fixed-path IPOPT implementation and 513 collocation points with adaptive refinements. The per-task pages show all seven joint position, velocity, acceleration, jerk and torque profiles, normalized geometric path profiles, and end-effector path projections.

Research interpretation

Moderate and severe results are summarized separately. The severe regime is the critical test: a positive B→D effect means a changed C geometry retimed by the same fixed-path solver improved on B's original geometry. Gains do not establish that broad geometry search is exhausted. C nonconvergence, limited homotopy exploration, source-task representativeness, finite sampling, and omission of a full planner rerun under derated torque remain material limitations. No global optimality claim is made.
