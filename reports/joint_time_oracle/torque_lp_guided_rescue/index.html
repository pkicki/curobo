Torque-LP-dual guided geometric rescue

NOT EXECUTED. This is a committed research implementation and qualification protocol, not new experimental performance evidence. The LP-guided, local-random and global-random arms have not yet been benchmarked on the three frozen counterexamples. No rescue rate, runtime advantage or new native-stage attribution is established.

Question: Can the active-joint/dual signal of a necessary torque LP obstruction guide configuration-space search to a feasible alternative faster than uninformed waypoint perturbations under matched computational budgets?

Three qualified synthetic-actuator counterexamples from the previous frozen study form a development cohort. All arms begin from the SAME nominal native cubic geometry fitted to a quintic spline; no arm receives the previously successful W path. The LP-dual arm uses local finite-difference RNEA sensitivities at torque bottlenecks. Both random baselines share the same geometric representation, scene checks and constructive timing backend. True success requires independent physical+Scaled95 dense/adaptive validation.
